## Task A - Portfolio Analysis
We are tasked to analyze two engineering portfolios online and study their functionality. The one I chose hosted from GitHub belongs to Nathan Hoong
https://nhoong.github.io/work-experience.html 

a) Navigating Nathan's portfolio is very simple, which is especially useful for users who are unfamiliar with GitHub. His projects are presented through the interface reminiscent to that a company website rather than a coding software. n the top of his page there is obvious location of portfolio, work experience, resume, and external profiles such as LinkedIn. Individual engineering projects are grouped by title and can be located via scrolling rather than having to go through file structures. This makes his portfolio accessible to engineering students who'd like a good example or recruiters who need to locate a project quickly. 

b) This portfolio provides enough information to understand its purpose as well as general function. We can clearly see that this portfolio belongs to that of an engineer, however, his documentation of projects is not consistent throughout. To reproduce the work without any additional information. For example, the Wobbler Engine provides a video demonstration as to how it runs. but the video is not accessible and says "the file you have requested does not exist". Whereas the example right above, The LabVIEW Code of
Automated Test Fixture, is able to describe what hardware was used as well as the objective of the project. So a colleague wanting to reproduce would likely need additional information

c) The portfolio doe snot explicitly show how decisions were made. Most projects are organized around an objective followed by the results. For example, the Shock Top Assembly project identifies the objective of allowing for the pivot of the shock an strut, and right underneath presents the result of how it was accomplished so no bending occurs. This communicates the design requirements and the final solution, it provides little information about calculations, design constraints, testing, or the selection of his design. 

d) The portfolio uses engineering terms and includes information appropriate for a employer. Nathan's projects are presented in a way that shows his engineering experience. Some of the noticeable engineering terminology is the "bill of materials" and "schematics". Numerical results are also included such as the ± 1% region of error in his Glaukos, Senior Capstone Project.
